# Business Process Automation — ERP, Invoicing & E-commerce

**Python · Enterprise Systems · Data Processing · Workflow Automation**

> **At a glance:** Developed business automations connecting ERP, invoicing and e-commerce workflows, focusing on data validation, controlled execution and reducing repetitive manual work.

---

## 1. Electronic Invoice Email Automation

**Challenge:** Customers were not systematically receiving electronic invoices through an automated email workflow.

**My contribution:** Developed and implemented an automated process for sending NF-e documents by email, establishing a more consistent customer communication workflow.

**Outcome:** The automation was completed, introducing a process that previously required a different approach. Delivery volumes and customer receipt rates are not presented as verified metrics.

## 2. ERP Order Processing — TOTVS & ViaShop

**Challenge:** Historical orders required controlled administrative processing to resolve pending records and support inventory availability updates.

**My contribution:** Developed automation for identifying eligible orders, applying business-rule validations, performing controlled processing and checking results.

**Outcome:** Historical pending records were processed, and subsequent changes in e-commerce availability were observed. Those changes cannot be attributed exclusively to the automation without accounting for other inventory movements.

## 3. Product Image Automation — ViaShop

**Challenge:** Associating product photographs with the correct references and variations required repetitive work and careful matching.

**My contribution:** Developed automated routines to match product images with references and variations, process uploads in batches and monitor execution results.

**Outcome:** A monitored batch of product images was processed, with successful uploads confirmed and remaining exceptions identified for follow-up.

---

## Engineering Focus

These projects reflect a shared engineering approach:

- Understanding operational rules before automating changes.
- Validating input data and identifying exceptions.
- Executing batch operations with safeguards.
- Checking results rather than assuming success.
- Preserving human oversight when needed.
