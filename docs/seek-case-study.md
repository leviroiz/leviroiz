# SEEK — Warehouse Inventory & Location Management

**Full-stack Development · Enterprise Software · Engineering Case Study**

> **At a glance:** I independently designed, developed and deployed SEEK, an internal warehouse system that helps employees locate products, organize storage positions and verify inventory through shared digital workflows.

**Role:** Sole Software Developer  
**Status:** Deployed · v1.5 (October 2026)  
**Stack:** React · TypeScript · Python · Django · PostgreSQL · Docker

---

## The Challenge

Warehouse operations depended heavily on employees' knowledge of product locations. This made locating items and coordinating assistance more difficult, particularly for colleagues unfamiliar with the storage layout.

The challenge was to make warehouse information accessible, structured and reliable.

## The Solution

I built SEEK to support day-to-day warehouse operations through:

- **Warehouse addressing:** structured storage locations and visual navigation.
- **Product location:** identification of storage positions for product variations.
- **Inventory verification:** operational workflows for checking and recording product presence.
- **Dashboard:** visibility into warehouse organization and addressing activities.
- **Traceability:** records of operational actions and responsible users.
- **Enterprise integration:** connection with existing product information.

## My Engineering Contributions

I was responsible for the application's architecture, database design, frontend, backend, business rules, testing and deployment.

Key engineering considerations included:

- **Data consistency:** validation and transactional database operations.
- **Concurrent changes:** safeguards against conflicting updates.
- **Operational reliability:** traceable actions and controlled error handling.
- **Release safety:** automated tests, backup verification and rollback planning.
- **Usability:** continuous feedback and validation with warehouse employees.

Operational teams provided requirements and feedback; I was responsible for the software implementation.

## Results

SEEK was deployed for internal operational use.

Warehouse employees reported better organization, easier product location and greater autonomy when helping with inventory activities.

These outcomes reflect qualitative feedback. Quantitative time savings and financial impact have not been formally measured.
